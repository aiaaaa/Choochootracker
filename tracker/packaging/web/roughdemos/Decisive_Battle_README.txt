DECISIVE BATTLE - NERV FM

A 150 BPM, eight-track remix of Shiro Sagisu's Evangelion cue.
80 bars / 2:08, plus a two-second audio-preview tail.

Open Decisive_Battle_NERV_FM.cct in the device personal build, select Song
row 00, then press Start. All percussion and other PCM is embedded in this
single file. No preset banks or external sample folders are needed.

Tracks 5-7 carry three simultaneous native Genesis FM / YM2612 horn voices.
Instruments 09, 0A, 0B are the low brass, horn body, and trumpets. Instrument
0C is the softer solo horn. These are editable four-operator algorithm-4
patches, with two parallel modulator/carrier pairs and native envelopes.
They are synthesized live by the FM engine, not sampled horns.

Track 1: tuned timpani ostinato
Track 2: kick and snare
Track 3: hats and crash
Track 4: sub bass
Tracks 5-7: FM brass section and horn lead
Track 8: string bed and metallic accents

Each Song row is an eight-bar section (12.8 seconds):
00 Timpani mobilization
01 FM brass entrance
02 Breakbeat assault
03 Brass octave variation
04 Half-time horn lead
05 Command-room breakdown
06 Rebuild
07 Final assault
08 High brass return
09 Withdrawal / final hit / stop

The arrangement is a reinterpretation, not an exact orchestral transcription.
PCM was synthesized for this project; no original soundtrack recording is used.
Original composition: Shiro Sagisu. Remix prepared for aiaaaa with Codex.
Pitch/rhythm reference consulted:
https://pianoletternotes.blogspot.com/2022/03/decisive-battle-by-shiro-sagisu-neon.html

Validation: headless render through the local ChooChooTracker engine, full
arrangement timing and stop checks, all eight tracks checked for output,
portable embedded-asset reload, and device transfer SHA-256 verification.
Direct listening and an interactive handheld audition remain for the user.
Generation source and validation records are retained beside this file.
