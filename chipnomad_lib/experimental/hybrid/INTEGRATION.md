Source: aiaaaa/hybrid-synth, commit b3c90fc2f2bea9afb994c166bc5177352e1cca35,
subdirectory hybrid-synth. Rev1 preserved engine include/src byte-for-byte. Rev2 adds an explicitly marked

No separate distribution license has been assigned to this component; its existing
provenance and notices are retained. The tracker adapter is outside this directory.
