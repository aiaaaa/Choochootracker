ChooChooTracker privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-10-11

This personal web build has no account system, advertising, analytics, or cloud-save service. Projects, imported presets, samples and settings are stored in this browser for this website. Import controls read local files without uploading them. Different browsers, devices and website domains have separate storage. Keep downloaded backups; clearing site data or browser storage eviction can remove local files.

When you choose Lucky NEXT, this site's hosting service requests a random-player page from The Mod Archive. Your browser then downloads the selected module directly from the archive. These requests disclose ordinary connection information to the respective hosting services. The relay accepts no song, preset or sample uploads and does not store your library. Choosing LOAD puts imported samples in your browser's local library.

Website updates replace the application, not your saved local library. Share a song by downloading and sending its project file explicitly.

This policy describes the personal fork. The upstream project is operated separately.
