FM master macros and native SID controls — October 5, 2026

FET envelope time, FTD modulator decay and FHR modulator coarse ratio are
available on OPLL, VRC7, OPL2, OPL3, Genesis, Arcade and DX7. FDT operator
detune spread and FLR/FLD native LFO rate/depth are available on Genesis,
Arcade and DX7. The LFO proposal is two independently editable commands.

All six FM commands are relative to the selected preset: 80 is unchanged.
They support SLE and modulation destinations, and clamp to native limits.
Existing native stepped selectors retain their small ranges. Help names their
parameter resolution separately from audio bit depth. Ratios/detune/LFO rates
remain quantized to native states; 00-FF macros do not create extra states.

Changes update live registers or voice parameters, without retriggering notes.
Yamaha zero-rate holds remain holds. Tone decay only affects modulators, so
fully additive patches have no response. Fixed-frequency DX7 operators retain
their frequency under FHR. LFO rate needs depth; amplitude modulation follows
operator enables/sensitivities. Positive FLD offsets enable a disabled native
Genesis/Arcade LFO. All values derive from the saved patch on every update.
Preset bytes are never modified by phrase automation.

SID adds SAT, SDE, SSU and SRL for native ADSR and SPR for its silent partner
ratio. All are discrete 00-0F; SPR selects ratios 1x-16x. The existing native
filter and voice budget remain. Sega/GB already expose the supported tone,
duty, noise, sweep and envelope controls; no invented waveform controls added.

Help now spells out preset-relative values, oscillator roles and SLE smoothing.
Native parameter ranges are documented, including 3-bit/eight-step controls.

Validation includes native phrase/voice equivalence without project mutation;
endpoint and neutral live automation across host block sizes; audible-response
fixtures with active decay/LFO; preservation of DX7 envelope stages; support
matrix/defaults; CNI, CCT and archive round trips. Production UI verifies every
new command has a title/description and value hint. Device validation adds an
FM macro audition to the SID and balanced sample/FM physical audio workloads.

Build/install results are recorded separately after validation completes.
